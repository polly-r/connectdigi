## The brief

Harbour & Hide make bags and small leather goods by hand in Cape Town. Most of their sales came through a market stall and word of mouth, and their online store was a lightly edited template that made a R6 000 bag look like a R600 one.

They wanted a store that felt as considered as the product, without adding steps between a customer and checkout.

## What we did

We started with the questions buyers asked at the stall: how the leather ages, what fits inside, how long delivery takes. Those answers shaped the product page, which leads with large, honest photography and puts materials, dimensions and care up front instead of in a tab nobody opens.

The rest of the site stays quiet. A short collection page, a journal for the workshop's stories, and a checkout trimmed to the fields that matter. We built it on a CMS the team can run themselves, so a new colourway goes live in minutes, not days.

## The result

The store now matches the price point. Orders moved from the market table to the website, fewer people drop out at checkout, and the team spends their time making bags instead of editing templates.
