## The brief

Ledgerline help small businesses send invoices and get paid on time. The product was good; the way they talked about it sounded like every other fintech. Their audience, owners of small service businesses, didn't want to learn accounting terms. They wanted to stop chasing money.

## What we did

We interviewed customers and wrote down the phrases they used, not the ones on the website. That became a tone of voice: plain, practical and slightly dry, like a good bookkeeper. From there we planned an article programme around real questions, such as when to send a reminder, what terms to set and what late payment actually costs.

The most useful articles became a downloadable guide, and the most common questions became short explainer videos, each under ninety seconds, that sales can drop into an email.

## The result

Prospective customers now arrive having read the guide, sales calls start further along, and the team has a library to add to rather than a blank page each month.
