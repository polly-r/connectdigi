## The brief

Mokone Events plans and styles weddings, corporate events, birthdays, launches and private parties, from décor and florals to marquees, tents and on-the-day management. Their work is personal and best shown, not described, and they wanted one place that showed its full range and gave people a simple way to start planning.

## What we did

We kept it to a single page, so a visitor reads one story from top to bottom: the services, what the team believes, the founder's story, the work and what clients say, ending at the enquiry form.

The six services sit together near the top, so a couple planning a wedding and a company planning a launch both see themselves straight away. The enquiry form asks for the type of event first, from wedding to private party, so every enquiry arrives already sorted, with the visitor's consent recorded. For people who would rather talk, WhatsApp and Instagram are one tap away.

## The result

Mokone Events now has one address to share on every quote, invitation and Instagram post, and every enquiry starts with the details the team needs to reply.
