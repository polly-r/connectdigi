## The brief

Tafel run three small bakeries with loyal regulars and a Saturday queue down the street. Their social media was an afterthought: beautiful bread, photographed badly, posted whenever someone had a minute.

They wanted more people to know what was on the shelf each morning, and a way to take pre-orders without another phone line.

## What we did

We built the account around the bake schedule instead of a marketing calendar. Every day has a format: what's fresh at 7am, the week's special, and Saturday's sourdough. Each story ends with an order link, so a post becomes a collection slot rather than just a like.

We shot a month of photography in each shop in one day, trained the front-of-house team to capture quick stories in the same style, and handled comments and messages so questions about allergens and wholesale never went unanswered.

## The result

Regulars now check the stories before they leave home, more pre-orders arrive through Instagram than the phone, and café and restaurant owners have started asking about wholesale in the DMs.
