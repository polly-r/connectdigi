## The brief

Singabenguni Consulting Services is a 100% woman-owned consultancy, founded in 2014, working on asset management, facilities, condition assessment and project delivery for public and private clients. The people who hire them are engineers, facilities managers and municipal officials who check credentials before they make contact. The firm needed a website that explains specialist services in plain language and makes its approach easy to verify.

## What we did

Five pages, each with one job. Home introduces the firm. About sets out its values and leadership. Services explains eight service lines, from strategy and asset management aligned to ISO 55000, IFRS and GIAMA, to facilities, project management, plumbing and construction. The gallery shows projects on the ground, and Contact takes the enquiry.

The enquiry form asks what kind of help is needed first, from condition assessment to facilities management, so each enquiry reaches the right expertise with context. The tone is professional and direct, and a LinkedIn link sits in the footer for anyone checking who leads the firm.

## The result

Prospective clients can see what the firm does, how it works and who leads it before they call, and every enquiry arrives sorted by the service it needs.
